  How FFmpeg aligns first samples (the canonical approach)

  decklink_dec.cpp doesn't actually "align" anything — it trusts DeckLink to do the pairing and just records each packet's hardware timestamp verbatim. The whole

  And the PTS sources (default for each stream):

  - Audio: audioFrame->GetPacketTime(&bmd_pts, time_base.den) — line 643

  The critical detail: GetStreamTime (video) and GetPacketTime (audio) return values in the SAME clock domain — DeckLink's stream clock, zeroed at
  StartStreams(). They are directly comparable. That's why no trim/pad is needed: each packet carries its true position in stream time, and the player/muxer
  schedules them correctly.

  There is one subtle bit of synchronization: initial_video_pts and initial_audio_pts are tracked separately. The first video frame anchors video at PTS=0; the
  first audio packet anchors audio at PTS=0. This works because in VideoInputFrameArrived DeckLink delivers video+audio for the same interval in the same
  callback — so both initial PTS values are essentially equal (within microseconds), and the cross-stream offset comes out near zero in the file.
